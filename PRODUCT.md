# DEN Global
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Consumers browse organizations and follow orders. Managers manage stores. Riders, dispatchers, transport operators, shipment and warehouse teams handle deliveries. Administrators review and manage access. Pet owners discover reviewed services.

## Product Purpose
One DEN account connects shopping, businesses, delivery operations and pet care. Preserve the existing permissions and functional workflows during UI changes.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React/Vite and vanilla JS workspaces share a same-origin API. Email verification is currently unconfigured; live payments and international bookings are disabled. Do not invent operational data or activate backend features as part of this redesign.

## Brand Commitments
DEN Global. “One network. Every journey.” The user selected the supplied Medusa field-simulator reference adapted to DEN delivery networks and parcels, retaining the project's green identity. Pinterest is supporting inspiration.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing application components, nine workspaces, and design-preview screenshots. Synthetic data must remain clearly identified.

## Product Principles
Preserve access boundaries. Make status and next actions clear. Keep operational screens readable on mobile. Use one consistent visual language.
